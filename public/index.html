How to comment

  1. Open a variation and press Add comment in the bar at the bottom (or the C key).
  2. Click the exact spot you want to comment on, type, and press Post. The first time you are asked for your name.
  3. Click a numbered pin to reply or mark it Resolved. Comments lists everything on that page.

The quote form is a mockup: nothing you type in it is sent anywhere.
